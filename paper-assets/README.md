# Source paper excerpts

These crops preserve the typesetting in the authors’ **IRIS Final Paper(1).pdf**, dated October 12, 2026. The complete source PDF and the personal recordings are not published in this repository.

- `title.png`: title, authors and opening abstract, page 1.
- `interaction.png`: primary spectator panel of Fig. 3, page 10; the printed coefficient annotation is excluded. The main-text interval −0.0033 to +0.0049 is typeset separately.
- `coverage-*.png`: the probe spectator, probe feedforward and GHZ parity panels of Fig. 5, page 11. These are the three observables whose five original intervals all miss the ideal.
- `equation.png`: original Eq. (7), page 3.
- `page-one.png`: first-page overview for document motion.

The plotted proof pair is illustrative and follows Section II C exactly: E = exp(−0.05λ), Ec = exp(−0.05λ − 0.02(λ−1)(λ−3)), with G=L=0. Both coincide at λ=1 and λ=3, but their zero coordinates are 1 and exp(−0.06).
